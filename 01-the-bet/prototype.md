# The Prototype Bet

## What I Built
A demand-planning Copilot that prioritizes high-impact forecast exceptions, explains the evidence behind each flag, and suggests the next investigation step without changing the forecast automatically.

## Tool Used
Bolt

## Prototype Link
https://shelfsense-ai-copilo-udma.bolt.host

## AI Value Archetype
Copilot

## The Bet in One Sentence
We are building an AI Copilot for CPG demand planners that reduces time spent investigating high-impact forecast exceptions while keeping the planner in control of consequential decisions.

## Pilot North Star
**High-value forecast exceptions resolved per planner-hour**

Supporting measures:
- planner investigation time;
- precision of high-priority flags;
- recommendation usefulness;
- planner adoption;
- willingness to pay for continued use.

## Kill Criteria
Kill or materially pivot the bet if, after two complete planning cycles with design partners:

- planner investigation time improves by **less than 15%**;
- high-priority exception precision is **below 80%**;
- the system produces any repeated material unsupported claims after remediation;
- fewer than **2 of 3** design partners indicate willingness to continue as paying AI customers; or
- the Copilot does not generate enough structured correction feedback to strengthen ShelfSense's learning loop.

The prototype is intended to validate workflow value and trust, not autonomous forecast execution.
