# ShelfSense — Product Overview

## Company Overview

**ShelfSense Technologies** is a fictional B2B SaaS company serving medium and large Consumer Packaged Goods (CPG) organizations across North America and the UK. The company helps commercial and supply-chain teams manage demand planning, promotional forecasting, and cross-functional forecast alignment.

ShelfSense currently serves **42 enterprise CPG customers** with approximately **$18.6M in Annual Recurring Revenue**. Its customers operate across categories such as food and beverage, personal care, home care, nutrition, pet care, and packaged goods.

---

## Product Overview

ShelfSense is an existing **demand and promotion planning platform** used by CPG companies to consolidate the data and assumptions required to create an operational demand forecast.

The platform integrates information such as:

- historical sales;
- inventory;
- retailer forecasts;
- promotional plans;
- product information;
- planner adjustments;
- forecast-versus-actual performance.

Demand planners use ShelfSense to review forecasts, incorporate sales and promotional inputs, manually adjust demand assumptions, and create the approved forecast used by supply chain and S&OP teams.

ShelfSense has successfully reduced dependence on disconnected spreadsheets and created a common planning workflow. However, the product still depends heavily on planners to manually identify anomalies, investigate different signals, determine which forecasts require attention, and explain why forecasts should change.

ShelfSense is therefore **not an AI-native product**. The strategic initiative explored in this case study investigates whether AI can be incorporated into the existing platform to create a fundamentally better planning experience.

---

## The Business Problem

The main problem ShelfSense wants to solve is not simply poor forecasting accuracy.

CPG demand planners operate in an environment where they must continuously interpret large amounts of fragmented information across thousands of products, retailers, promotions, and locations.

ShelfSense customer research indicates that planners spend a significant portion of their time:

- identifying which forecasts require attention;
- reconciling different sources of information;
- investigating unexpected demand changes;
- assessing promotional assumptions;
- manually adjusting forecasts;
- explaining forecast changes to other stakeholders.

In the case-study baseline, approximately **47% of planner time is spent investigating and reconciling information**, while only **22% is spent directly making planning decisions**.

This creates a broader **decision-quality and decision-efficiency problem**.

---

## Why This Matters

Forecasting decisions directly affect inventory, production, retailer availability, and promotional performance.

Overforecasting can lead to:

- excess inventory;
- higher warehousing costs;
- markdowns;
- product expiry;
- working-capital pressure.

Underforecasting can result in:

- stockouts;
- lost sales;
- retailer penalties;
- missed promotional opportunities.

For large CPG organizations, relatively small improvements in planning decisions can therefore create significant financial value.

---

## Strategic Opportunity

ShelfSense sees an opportunity to move beyond being a system where planners **prepare forecasts** and become a platform that helps organizations **make better demand decisions**.

The strategic opportunity is:

> **Determine whether AI can materially improve the speed, quality, and economic impact of CPG demand decisions while maintaining the trust, control, and reliability required by enterprise planning teams.**

The project deliberately does not begin with a predefined AI solution.

Instead, the strategy work will determine:

- where AI can create the greatest customer value;
- which planning decisions should remain human-led;
- what level of reliability is required;
- whether customers will pay for the additional value;
- whether ShelfSense can build a defensible advantage;
- whether the economics support operating AI at enterprise scale.

---

## Product Vision

### Vision

**Help CPG organizations make better commercial and supply decisions by turning fragmented market signals into trusted demand decisions.**

The vision intentionally focuses on **decision quality rather than forecast generation alone**.

ShelfSense ultimately wants to become the decision layer connecting commercial demand signals with operational planning.

---

## Product Mission

**Give every CPG planning team a shared, continuously improving view of future demand that they can understand, challenge, and act on.**

The product experience should therefore be:

**Shared** — Sales, marketing, finance, and supply chain work from aligned demand assumptions.

**Improving** — The system becomes better as it learns from previous forecasts, planner decisions, and actual outcomes.

**Actionable** — Insights help users make business decisions rather than simply generate reports.

---

## Primary Customer and User

### Target Customer

ShelfSense primarily targets CPG organizations with:

- $500M–$8B in annual revenue;
- thousands of active SKUs;
- multiple retailers and channels;
- significant promotional activity;
- dedicated demand-planning teams;
- complex ERP and planning environments.

### Primary User

The primary user is the **Demand Planner**.

Demand planners are responsible for consolidating information from sales, marketing, historical demand, promotions, and supply chain into the operational forecast used by the organization.

Supporting users include:

- Key Account / Sales Managers;
- Trade Marketing teams;
- Supply Planners;
- Finance and S&OP stakeholders.

---

## Representative Customer

For the case study, ShelfSense will use a fictional enterprise customer called **EverGlow Consumer Products**.

EverGlow is a **$2.4B CPG organization** operating across Canada and the United States with:

- 3,800 active SKUs;
- 27 retail customers;
- 14 demand planners;
- approximately 4,200 promotions annually;
- $310M in annual trade-promotion investment.

EverGlow currently operates at approximately **71% overall forecast accuracy** and **58% promotional forecast accuracy**, while manually reviewing thousands of SKU-level forecasts each month.

The company therefore represents a realistic environment in which improvements to planning decisions could create substantial financial impact.

---

## Current Product Position

ShelfSense already possesses several assets that could potentially support an AI strategy:

- historical sales data;
- promotional history;
- forecasts and actual outcomes;
- inventory information;
- planner forecast overrides;
- established planning workflows;
- enterprise integrations;
- relationships with 42 CPG customers.

However, significant constraints also exist:

- customer data cannot automatically be pooled for shared model training;
- datasets vary substantially between customers;
- historical data quality is inconsistent;
- planners have relatively low trust in automated recommendations;
- AI capabilities may introduce additional infrastructure cost and governance requirements.

The strategy must therefore determine whether these assets can produce a sustainable competitive advantage rather than simply another AI feature.

---

## North Star

ShelfSense's proposed North Star is:

### **Value-Adjusted Demand Decisions per Planner**

Rather than measuring AI usage, prompts, or recommendations generated, the metric focuses on whether the platform helps planners make **more valuable decisions with less effort**.

Supporting metrics may include:

- planner investigation time;
- planning-cycle duration;
- forecast accuracy;
- promotional forecast accuracy;
- recommendation acceptance;
- inventory reduction;
- lost-sales reduction;
- planner confidence.

The exact measurement model will be validated as the strategy develops.

---

## Strategic Ambition

ShelfSense's three-year ambition is:

> **Move from being a system planners use to prepare forecasts into a system organizations rely on to make demand decisions.**

Achieving this could potentially improve:

- customer retention;
- contract expansion;
- product differentiation;
- workflow adoption;
- proprietary learning;
- long-term competitive defensibility.

---

## AI Product Strategy Question

The central question guiding the certification case study is:

> **Where, if anywhere, can ShelfSense use AI to create sufficiently valuable, defensible, trusted, and economically sustainable improvements to CPG demand planning?**
